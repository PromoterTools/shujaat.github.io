I am a Postdoctoral Researcher and Machine Learning Group Lead at Shenzhen University, China. I hold a PhD in Artificial Intelligence from Jeonbuk National University, South Korea, where I was supported by the prestigious Brain Korea (BK) Fellowship in 2023. My master's and bachelor's degrees in Computer Sciences are from Bahria University, Islamabad, Pakistan.

My research sits at the intersection of deep learning and computational genomics. During my PhD I independently built nine state-of-the-art tools for regulatory sequence prediction — specifically promoter identification and classification — across bacteria, yeast, rice, maize, phage, and archaea, each deployed as a publicly accessible web server. A unifying theme across this work is explainability: I integrate SHAP and motif analysis to ground model predictions in biologically meaningful sequence features.

In my current postdoctoral role I have expanded into protein language models (ESM, ProtBERT), transformer-based architectures for sequence representation, and generative AI pipelines (VAEs, diffusion models) for novel enzyme discovery.
